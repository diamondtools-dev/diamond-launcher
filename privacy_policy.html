Privacy Policy

App: Daily Diamond Launcher • Package: com.diamondtools.launcher

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Welcome to Daily Diamond Launcher ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data and permissions when you use our mobile application.

Summary: We do NOT collect personal identity information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts, or passwords. Data processed on your device (such as installed app lists) stays locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Our application operates with a privacy-first approach:

  • Locally Processed Data: To function as an Android Home Launcher, the application accesses the list of installed applications on your device strictly to render your app drawer and allow you to launch your apps. This data is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Automated Diagnostic & Usage Data: We may receive non-personally identifiable diagnostic, crash, and usage statistics via third-party SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics) to improve app stability and user experience.

2. Android Device Permissions

To provide full launcher and utility functionality, the application requests the following standard Android permissions:

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Required as a core function for Home Screen Replacement (Launcher) applications to detect, index, and launch your installed applications.
  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES: Used only when you explicitly select the "Uninstall" action for an application via the context menu.
  • SET_WALLPAPER / Wallpaper Access: Used to display your chosen system wallpaper behind the transparent launcher interface.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load real-time strategy guides, remote configuration settings, and serve advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

We utilize reputable third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device anonymously for analytics and advertising purposes. Below are the links to their respective privacy policies:

Google AdMob may use standard advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and cookies to serve relevant, policy-compliant non-disruptive ads. You can manage or reset your advertising identifier via your Android device Settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
